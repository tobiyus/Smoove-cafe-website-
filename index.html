Hello, neighbour

A little cafe with a lot of heart

Smoove is a family-run neighbourhood cafe in Melksham. Everything is made to order in our small kitchen, from the first flat white of the morning to the last smoothie before we close.

Come in after a session, after the school run, or just because it's Tuesday. Bring a friend, bring the laptop, bring the dog. Nobody's rushing you out.
