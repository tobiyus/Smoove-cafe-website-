Privacy notice

Your details, looked after

Plain English, no small print. Last updated 21 November 2026.

Who we are

Smoove Cafe is a family-run cafe inside City Fitness gym hub, . We're the "data controller" for the details you give us when you book a table, which means we decide how they're used and we're responsible for keeping them safe.

You can contact us about anything on this page at .

What we collect when you book

  • Your name
  • Your phone number and/or email address
  • The date, time, length and size of your booking
  • Anything you choose to add in the notes, such as allergies, needing a high chair or bringing a dog

If you tell us about an allergy or a health condition in the notes, we use it only so our team can look after you on the day. Please only share what you're happy for us to know. You can always tell us in person instead.

Why we use it

We use your details only to take and manage your booking: to hold your table, send your confirmation and a reminder email the day before, contact you if something changes, and let you cancel. The legal basis is that it's needed to provide the booking you've asked for (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). For anything you add in the notes about allergies or health, we rely on your explicit consent when you choose to tell us (Article 9(2)(a)).

We never sell your details, never add you to a mailing list and never use them for marketing.

Work & Events enquiries

If you send us an enquiry about team lunches or events, we collect your name, email, and anything else you choose to give us: company, phone number, dates, numbers, delivery postcode, budget, dietary needs and your message. We use it only to reply to you, send a quote and arrange your order. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and your explicit consent for anything you tell us about allergies or health (Article 9(2)(a)).

We automatically remove your personal details from our list of enquiries 12 months after we receive them. If you go on to place an order, we keep what we need for our accounts, such as invoices, for as long as the law requires.

Where it's stored

Bookings and enquiries are stored in a private Google Sheet in the cafe's own Google account, protected by a password and two-step sign-in. Confirmation emails are sent through the same Google account. Google acts as our "processor" under its data processing terms and may store data on servers outside the UK, protected by the safeguards the law requires.

Only the cafe owners and staff who need to manage bookings can see them. The website itself (hosted by GitHub Pages) doesn't store your booking details. GitHub may log basic technical details such as your IP address to keep the site secure.

How long we keep it

We automatically remove your name, phone number, email address and notes 30 days after your booking date. We keep the anonymous part (date, time and number of people) to help us plan staffing.

Cookies

Our website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. The map on our Find us page is provided by Google and only loads if you choose to show it. Google may set cookies when it does. Our staff booking page uses your browser's temporary storage to keep staff signed in until the tab is closed.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your details, ask us to correct or delete them, object to how we use them, or withdraw your consent for anything you shared in the notes. Just ask us in the cafe, . We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, please talk to us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
